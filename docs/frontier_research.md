## Owned supplemental program

The private pipeline independently reconstructs or separately names analogues for AP polling, dated BartTorvik, ESPN game-specific predictor context, multi-provider market context, venue/context summaries, coach history, and player-value/participation proxies.

Exact proprietary KenPom/EvanMiya values and complete contemporaneous historical injury data remain explicit gaps. Season-level ESPN BPI was captured but blocked from historical modeling when endpoint update timing was after tournament cutoffs.

See [supplemental-source ownership](supplemental_source_ownership.md) for the public matrix.

## Validation conclusion

Broad public-solution replacements repeatedly showed unstable transfer. The retained improvement in this lineage came from a narrow men-only correction that preserves already strong rows rather than replacing the full system.

The public repository intentionally does not expose the exact private gates, weights, candidate prediction bytes, model binaries, identity logic, or AWS orchestration.

## Coverage and prospective capture

The historical data scope covers **men 2003+ / women 2010+**.

For 2027, unavailable source inputs remain waiting states. New observations are intended to be captured prospectively with actual timestamps/checksums before feature eligibility is considered.
