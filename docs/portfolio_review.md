## Ten highest-impact gaps

1. **Measured optimization was missing:** V347 identified a large return but the public record did not yet show whether the repair worked. V348 now supplies observed runtime, request and return deltas.
2. **Disk release could be confused with logical bytes:** new action-sum and free-space-change contracts separate preserved source bytes, released blocks and observed free space.
3. **Cache aliases could exaggerate savings:** 303 removed aliases freed zero blocks; the record and tests keep that case visible.
4. **Negative-cache creation could be advertised as reuse:** 7,700 new checkpoints are explicitly distinct from zero negative-cache hits.
5. **Sequential runs could be mislabeled a controlled benchmark:** the schema records uncontrolled background/provider conditions and excludes an optimality claim.
6. **Archived-byte custody could be hidden:** body and restoration-manifest readback is required for the cleanup claim, and remaining unmanifested caches stay explicit.
7. **Frozen inventory and repeated price work remain expensive:** observed phases still consume 135.18 and 31.12 seconds; optimizing those belongs to the private runtime and needs owner measurement.
8. **Market qualification is incomplete:** 7,768 broader unknown containers and 7,700 attempted deterministic failures are different populations; no purchase or training eligibility follows from either.
9. **Branch protection and independent accessibility certification remain absent:** green workflow results do not create repository enforcement or certify every assistive technology.
10. **No prospective scientific result:** same accepted control and already-exposed evaluation population; faster replay does not establish a new competitive gain.

## Public and private boundary

Public reproduction remains `python tools/review_project.py` with Python 3.12 and Node. The public record contains aggregates and artifact digests. It contains no raw source bodies, cloud paths, credentials, per-game predictions, weights, exact private blends or feature recipes. The publication scan and nested allowlist provide concrete checks; they do not replace every possible license review or authenticate private publishers independently. No new third-party dataset is distributed.

## What would earn the remaining improvement

They cannot be resolved by assigning the target score or repeating a successful retrospective pipeline.
