## Three things to evaluate

**Research judgment:** the project stopped unsuccessful directions and tested a
materially different supervised target. A matched win/loss control distinguishes
score-margin information from merely adding another model. The latest women's
extension improves the historical core in all three years, but loses to the matched
binary control in 2025; that qualification is visible rather than hidden.

**ML engineering:** experiments enforce prediction-time boundaries, validated joins,
probability/schema constraints, content-hashed artifacts, reproducible private
state, checkpoint recovery and bounded compute. Submission logic protects unchanged
rows and avoids automatic repeated uploads after ambiguous network responses.

**Communication:** results have explicit populations and provenance. The scored
incumbent remains separate from a research candidate, and the 2027 plan prioritizes
prospective evaluation rather than treating repeated 2026 feedback as a fresh test.
