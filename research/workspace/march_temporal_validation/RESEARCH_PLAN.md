## Domain mechanism and sources

Pomeroy's Ratings Glossary explains offense/defense in points per possession and adjustment for opposition. That motivates distinct offensive and defensive changes rather than unadjusted recent wins or points per game. It does not validate our day windows, shrinkage, or particular classifier.

- Ken Pomeroy, *Ratings Glossary*: https://kenpom.com/blog/ratings-glossary/
- Ken Pomeroy, *Ratings methodology update*: https://kenpom.com/blog/ratings-methodology-update/

Cawley and Talbot explain how selecting among finite-sample validation estimates can itself overfit. This is why the discovery years do not determine the new gate and why repeated historical use remains an explicit limitation, even when tournament labels never enter feature construction.

- Gavin C. Cawley and Nicola L. C. Talbot, 2010, *On Over-fitting in Model Selection and Subsequent Selection Bias in Performance Evaluation*, JMLR 11, 2079–2107: https://www.jmlr.org/beta/papers/v11/cawley10a.html

Sources motivate the design; they do not establish a future gain or guarantee a leaderboard record. All sources were consulted in this response; the mathematical definitions below come from the unchanged project implementation.

## Frozen feature definitions

An additive offense/opponent-defense/site ridge model was fitted using each season's regular games through **day 100**. It is already cached. That model does not see the later games it is used to assess.

For a later team-game:

```text
Offensive surprise = observed points per 100 possessions − frozen expected efficiency
Defensive surprise = frozen expected allowed efficiency − observed allowed efficiency
```

Positive defensive surprise means conceding less than the early model expected.

For each side separately, let R be the surprise:

```text
Window 1 = days 101–116
Window 2 = days 117–132
Shrunken window mean = sum(R) / (number of usable games + 5)
Change = shrunken mean in Window 2 − shrunken mean in Window 1
Matchup input = team A's Change − team B's Change
```

The two selected columns are:

```text
diff_late_offense_change
diff_late_defense_change
```

The 14-day recency weight belongs to the earlier **level** features, not these selected unweighted window contrasts. No new weighting is introduced here.

Previously fixed parameters: ridge alpha 20, maximum symmetric possession discrepancy 0.10, snapshot day 132, zero-centered five-game prior. Unknown early team/opponent matchups are excluded; support is recorded, not treated as observed zero performance. The stored early rating/snapshot association and all checksum receipts are verified.

## Leakage and information timing

Features use only each season's pre-tournament regular-season games. The two change features do not accept tournament outcomes. Anchor seeds require the announced field, so the prediction timestamp is after bracket release, not earlier. Features are assembled before the classifier split; no current tournament target is used to calculate them. Historical tournament labels for a validation year s are restricted to 2013 through s−1 for fitting. No 2026 labels enter this experiment.

The frozen early model can be stale, and opponents can improve too. Residual changes are not identified causal improvement, roster availability, injuries, or momentum. Variation in window support and shrinkage is another possible source of noise. These limitations remain even if scores improve.

## Predefined gate and conditional components

The gate considers only 2016/2018. Require both deltas < 0 and their equal-season mean <= -0.0005. These thresholds allocate compute; they are not p-values, confidence bounds, or claims of independent replication. Do not alter them based on the observed result.

If passed, test:

1. Anchor + defensive change (drop offensive change).
2. Anchor + offensive change (drop defensive change).

Each configuration is evaluated on all four years, giving at most eight new component fits. Their other inputs remain fixed. Positive `delta_vs_full` means removal worsened Brier. Because the gate selected this family, these are conditional, exploratory component comparisons, not unbiased automatic model selection.

If failed, components are skipped and the complete report is still generated. No computation auto-launches after this milestone. Maximum new classifiers across both stages: ten. A valid checkpoint is never deliberately refitted to satisfy a fit counter.

## Interpretation, validation limits, and next choices

All four years have already been used in other project research. The new gate excludes the exact discovery years but does not restore an untouched test set. Small samples, overlapping temporal training sets and prior multiple comparisons limit conclusions. The leave-one-season-out average and per-game ECDF are descriptive sensitivity plots, not confidence intervals treating games or seasons as independent.

A pass supports considering unchanged testing in a later historical era and transferring the candidate into the fixed stronger women's production recipe. No improvement to the submitted 0.1222672 is established by this reference-model test. A failure stops expansion of this implementation, without claiming all forms of temporal information or every classifier have been exhausted.

The wider feature-engineering backlog remains open: ranking coverage/disagreement, opponent-specific rebounding and turnover interactions, schedule-adjusted matchup representations, historically verifiable roster availability, and transfer of promising compact representations into the stronger fixed production recipes. None is launched or claimed completed here.
