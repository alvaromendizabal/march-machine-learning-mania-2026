What is exact here — and what is assumed?

Every route is included

The engine propagates matchup probabilities round by round. A team’s chance of advancing combines its own survival probability with the possible opponents and their chances of reaching that game. No random simulation is needed.

The assumptions stay simple

Ratings are fixed through the tournament. Matchup probabilities depend only on rating differences and the uncertainty scale. There are no injuries, schedules, player features, learned parameters or changing game conditions in this demo.

Keep the evidence separate

The school names are real. All ratings, bracket positions, prices and example outcomes are synthetic. These probabilities do not reproduce the project’s private historical forecast, and changing controls does not establish calibration or predictive performance.
